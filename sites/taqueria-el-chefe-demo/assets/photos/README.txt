Put real photos of Taqueria El Chefe food and place here.

How to use them (see commented <img> blocks in ../../index.html, menu section, three "Most ordered" tiles):
  - birria-pizza.jpg        -> large tile "Birria Pizza"
  - birria-tacos.jpg        -> tile "Birria Tacos"
  - birria-quesadilla.jpg   -> tile "Birria Quesadilla"
Use JPG/WebP about 1200x900 (4:3), under 200 KB each. Always add a real alt text.
To switch a tile to its photo, uncomment its <img class="photo"> line (the illustration hides itself).
Do not use stock photos of other restaurants' food.
